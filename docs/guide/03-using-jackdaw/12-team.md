# Workspaces and people

Give each team, site or client a workspace, put people in it, and share items with it. Talk with them in **Chats**. How the rules work: [Who can use a brain](../04-concepts/05-access-tiers.md).

## Switch workspace

The workspace switcher sits at the top of the menu (an icon on a phone). Pick a workspace and the lists show its items. **All my workspaces** shows everything you can read. Your private workspace is **Only you**.

Search always looks in all your workspaces. A result from another workspace carries its name, and opening it switches there.

## Make a workspace

1. Open **Settings > Workspaces** and click **New workspace**.
2. Enter a **Name** and, if you like, a description.
3. Click **Make workspace**. You are its first user and its Moderator.

On its screen you can change its colour and icon, and pick its landing page. An admin also sets its assistant and connectors there.

## Add people

1. Open the workspace in **Settings > Workspaces**.
2. Under **Add a user**, search by name or email and click **Add**.

They can read every item shared with the workspace straight away. Only people who already have a login show up: invite someone new first ([Add users](../05-admin/07-logins.md)).

A **Moderator** adds people, edits the workspace and changes the items whose home is that workspace. Only an admin turns the **Moderator** switch on or off.

## Share an item

1. Open the item and click **Access**. The panel lists its home and every workspace it is shared with.
2. Click **Add workspace** and pick one under **Share with**. You must be a Moderator of the item's home and of that workspace.
3. Leave **Writable** off to let people there read only. Turn it on to let them edit.

To stop sharing, click **Revoke** on that row. To move the item to another home, pick **Home** on that row; you can keep it readable in the old home.

When you move a folder to another home, the dialog also offers **Also move the N items inside**, ticked. The sub-folders and files below it that have the same home move with it, at any depth. An item below it stays where it is when it has another home, is of a kind the new home cannot hold, was **Removed here** for the new home (on it or on a folder it is in), or is in a folder you cannot open. The dialog says how many stay.

A folder shares what is in it: open **Access…** from the folder's menu in the tree. Before you confirm, the dialog says how many items it will share. If a page embeds items that are not shared the same way, the panel offers **Grant these too**.

## Pick the landing page

Each workspace has a landing page: what its people see at **Desk**. A Moderator picks **Desk** or a published app the workspace holds, under **Landing page**. See [Workspace home apps](../07-api/07-member-home-apps.md).

## Take access away

- **Remove a person**: click the remove button on their row. They lose the workspace's items at once, unless another of their workspaces has them. To leave a workspace, remove yourself.
- **Archive a workspace**: **Archive workspace** at the top of its screen. It takes the workspace's shares off every item. It is refused while items still have their home there: move them first.
- **End a login**: switch on **Disabled** in **Settings > Users**.

## Talk with your team

**Chats** holds direct chats and groups between people. You can start a chat with anyone who shares a workspace with you. Click **+**, pick a person, type and send. For a group, pick **New group**, give it a **Group name** and its members. See [Chats](../06-help/chats.md).

Nobody reads a chat they are not in, admins and the owner included.

## Chat with a workspace's assistant

Each workspace can have one assistant, set in its **Assistant** section. It reads only that workspace's items. In **Chats**, click **+**, then pick the assistant under **Agents**. Everyone has their own thread with it, and nobody else reads that thread, admins and the owner included.
