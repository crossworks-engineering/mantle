# Profile and appearance

Set your timezone, writing rules and reminders in **Settings > Profile**, and the look of Jackdaw in **Settings > Appearance**.

## Profile

Open **Settings > Profile**, change what you need, and save. The line at the top shows today's date and time in your current settings, so you can check the format.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Photo**, **Avatar** | Your picture in the header. A photo wins over the generated avatar. |
| **Site name**, **Peer name** | The name in the header's top left, and the brain's peer identity in the header centre. |
| **Speciality**, **What this brain is for** | The brain's kind, and a sentence or two on its main use. Every assistant reads that sentence at the start of each conversation. |
| **House style** | Your writing rules, in your own words, for example "never use exclamation marks". Every agent follows them in replies, pages, tables and emails. |
| **Timezone (IANA)** | What "tomorrow at 3pm" means, when quiet hours start, and how dates show. For example `Africa/Johannesburg`. |
| **Locale (BCP-47)** | Date, number and currency format. For example `en-GB`. It does not change the reply language. |
| **Reminder delivery** | Telegram or the mobile app. |
| **Event reminders from** | Which agent's Telegram bot sends reminders. |
| **Live thinking & streaming** | Watch the assistant's steps and the reply as it is written. |
| **Thinking effort** | How hard agents set to **Inherit** reason before they answer. Needs live thinking on. Higher costs more. |

Get the timezone right first. A wrong timezone raises no error, but every reminder lands at the wrong hour. You can change it in chat while you travel: "I'm in London this week, update my timezone."

## Appearance

Open **Settings > Appearance**. Changes apply as you click.

- **Mode**: **Light**, **Dark** or **System**.
- **Color theme**: a full palette for the whole app. Filter the list by name.
- **Fonts**: the interface font, the wordmark, the peer name, and the font for pages and notes, each with its own size.
- **Avatar style** and **Logo**: how generated avatars are drawn, and your brand mark.
- **Backgrounds**: generated backdrops for the menu, header, chat and activity column. Each can be off. They take their colours from the theme.

Pick a theme by contrast. Success, warning and error colours should be easy to tell apart.

## Password and sign-in

Change your password, and see or end signed-in devices, on **Settings > Logins**. See [Member and client logins](../05-admin/07-logins.md).
