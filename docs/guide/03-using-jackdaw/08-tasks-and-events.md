# Tasks and events

Track what you need to do in **Tasks**, and put dated things with reminders in **Events**.

## Tasks

A task has a title, a status, a priority and an optional due date. Statuses are **To do**, **In progress**, **Blocked** and **Done**.

1. Open **Tasks** and create a task.
2. Fill in **Title**, **Priority** and **Due**, then save.
3. Open the task to add a **Checklist** of steps, and keep progress in its **Notes**.

Switch between the list and the board with the view toggle. On the board, drag a card to change its status. Blocked tasks sit in the **In progress** column with a badge.

The quickest way is to let tasks come out of conversation:

- "Add a task: order the replacement seal, high priority, due Friday."
- "What's overdue?"
- "Mark the seal task done."

Tasks never notify you on their own. For a reminder, use an event. For a recurring nudge, use a [heartbeat](15-heartbeats.md).

## Events

An event has a start, an optional end, a location, a reminder and a repeat rule.

1. Open **Events** and create an event.
2. Fill in **Title** and **Starts**.
3. Pick **Remind**: at start, 5 or 15 minutes, 1 hour or 1 day before.
4. Pick **Repeat** if it recurs, and an optional **Until** date.
5. Save.

Or ask: "Put the site inspection in for Tuesday at 9, remind me an hour before."

An event's page has **Add to calendar**, which downloads an `.ics` file.

### Where reminders arrive

Reminders go to Telegram or the mobile app. Set this under **Reminder delivery** in **Settings > Profile**. By default a reminder follows the surface you last messaged from.

Times use your profile timezone, so "tomorrow at 3pm" means your 3pm. See [Profile and appearance](16-profile.md).

### Subscribed calendars

Calendars you subscribe to in **Settings > Calendars** show here too. They are read-only copies. Change those events where they live.

## If it fails

- **No reminder arrived**: pair Telegram or the mobile app first. See [Connect Telegram](../02-first-steps/05-connect-telegram.md).
- **Everything is a few hours off**: your profile timezone is wrong.
