## Appearance

Appearance sets how MantleOS looks. Most choices apply as you click.

- **Mode**: Light, Dark or System.
- **Color theme**: pick one of about forty full palettes. Type in the filter box to find one by name.
- **Fonts**: set the **Interface font**, the **Wordmark** (the brain's name in the header), **Pages and Notes** (the editor, shared pages and PDF export) and the **Peer name**, each with its own size.
- **Avatar style**: the look of every generated avatar, and how much theme colour it takes (**Original**, **Mixed** or **Theme**).
- **Logo**: upload one for **Light mode** and one for **Dark mode** (SVG, PNG, JPEG or WebP, under 512KB). Without a dark logo, the light one is used in both.
- **Background generator**: one animated background for the whole brain. Click **Generate** until you like one, pick a shape (**Mesh**, **Silk**, **Aurora**, **Dunes** or **Marble**), a tone (**Darker**, **Auto** or **Lighter**) and the **Motion**, then **Save background**. **Remove** takes it away. Inside the app it stays close to the page colour so text on it keeps reading. The sign-in, invite and shared pages show it in full colour, with their text on a solid card.
- **Drift lab**: the link under the generator opens every option of that background (strength, scale, turbulence, softness, depth, grain, angle, brand or chart colours) with a live preview. **Save background** there saves the same background.
- **Shared surfaces**: the mode a share link opens in (visitors can still switch), and a switch to turn the background off on shared pages so they print plain. Both save as you click.
- **Backgrounds**: a generated backdrop for the **Menu**, **Brand**, **Chat** and **Activity** areas. Each can be **Off**. Only the menu has one at first.

Pick a theme by contrast as well as colour. The status colours (success, warning, error) need to stand apart so you spot a failed job at a glance.

## Assistant

The assistant cannot change appearance. Set it here.

## Technical

- Colours are theme tokens. Each surface asks for a role such as background, card or border, and the theme supplies the colour. Backgrounds take their colours from the theme too.
- Mode is stored in this browser. Theme, fonts, logo and backgrounds are stored on the brain, so they apply to everyone who signs in.
- Fonts are variable fonts, downloaded only when something uses them.
- If a screen looks unstyled after an update, reload the page.
