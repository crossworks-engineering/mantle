# Talk to the assistant

Open the assistant and ask it something. It answers from your brain and can do things for you.

## Open it

Click **Assistant** at the top of the menu, or press Cmd+I (Ctrl+I on Linux and Windows). It opens as a side column. The **...** menu in its header switches it to a movable window or the full screen.

## Send a message

1. Type in the message box and press Enter.
2. To speak instead, click the microphone (**Record voice note**). Your words are written into the box for you to check and send.
3. To share a file, click the paperclip (**Attach images or documents**). The assistant reads it for its answer and also files it into your brain.

A first message to try: "What can you help me with?"

## Ask it to do things

The assistant has tools, so plain requests work:

- "Make a note that the gate code is 4821."
- "Add a task to renew the car licence."
- "Remind me on Friday at 3pm to call the plumber."
- "Turn this into a page."

Any tool you have set to need approval waits under **Pending** until you approve it.

## Start over

Use **New chat** in the header for a fresh conversation. **Previous chats** in the **...** menu opens earlier ones. Your memory stays the same in every chat.
