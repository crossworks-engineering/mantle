# Licensing

Mantle is free to self-host and run for your own use under the Functional Source License (FSL-1.1-MIT). A paid commercial license is needed only to sell it or build it into a product that competes with it.

## What you may do without paying

The license allows any purpose except a competing use. That includes:

- running Mantle for yourself or inside your company;
- non-commercial education and research;
- professional services for someone who uses Mantle under the same license.

You may use, copy, change and redistribute the code for these purposes.

## What needs a commercial license

A competing use: offering Mantle, or something that does substantially the same job, to others as a commercial product or service. Two common cases:

- **Embedded use**: shipping Mantle's code, APIs or database schema inside your own paid software.
- **Competing service**: hosting Mantle and selling access to it.

For a commercial license, email licensing@crossworks.engineering.

## Each version becomes MIT after two years

Two years after a version is first published, that version is also available under the MIT license. Newer versions stay under the FSL until their own two years pass.

## Third-party software

Mantle includes open-source packages under their own licenses, mostly MIT and Apache 2.0. Every license text is in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` in the repository.

## The legal texts

This page is a summary. Where it differs from the license files, the files apply.

- `LICENSE.md`: the full FSL text.
- `LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md`: the commercial agreement.
- `LICENSING.md`: a plain-language explainer.

The Jackdaw interface is licensed the same way, with the same files in its own repository.
