# 0.205.6 (2026-07-26)

**A brand palette you can actually read.** Settings → Appearance gains
**Pinnacle**, the first hand-authored colour theme in a picker otherwise filled
with imported tweakcn presets, and the first with its contrast pinned by a
test.

## Added

### The Pinnacle theme (light + dark)

Built from the eight Pinnacle brand colours, `#ea3635` `#ec1c35` `#d48b38`
`#b2d234` `#6fbe44` `#6fc173` `#6ec7aa` `#6ec8b7`.

Every one of them is light-key: white text fails WCAG AA on all eight, the best
case being `#ec1c35` at 4.39:1 and the worst `#b2d234` at 1.73:1. Dropped
straight into `--primary` or `--destructive` they produce exactly the washed-out
result the picker's swatches would promise and the UI wouldn't deliver. So the
palette splits the difference:

- **Brand hexes ship unchanged where nothing sits on top of them**,
  `--chart-1..5` carries all eight across the two modes, and dark mode's
  `--primary` is `#6fbe44` itself, over near-black ink.
- **Fills that carry text use hue-exact shades**: scaled toward black in
  *linear* light, so chromaticity is identical and only luminance moves.
  `--primary: #457928` is `#6fbe44`; `--destructive: #d5182f` is `#ec1c35`.

The binding constraint is that these tokens do double duty: `text-primary` and
`text-destructive` appear ~590 times, so each is both a fill *under* its
`-foreground` and ink *on* `--background`/`--card`/`--sidebar`. A mid-tone
satisfies neither direction. The sidebar (the darkest light-mode surface) sets
the ceiling in light mode; dark mode runs the other way, lifting destructive to
`#ee5d66` so `text-destructive` reads on a dark card, which in turn puts dark ink
on the button, matching how the green primary already works there.

Tightest measured pairs: **4.67:1** light (`primary` on sidebar), **5.04:1** dark
(`destructive` on card). Everything else lands between 5:1 and 15:1.

## Changed

### `themes.test.ts`: the contrast contract

New unit test asserting, per mode, 24 ink/surface pairs at AA text contrast
(4.5:1) and three ring pairs at non-text contrast (3:1), plus that the picker
swatches match the real CSS values. It caught four failures in the first draft
of the palette, including the sidebar case above.

Only Pinnacle is asserted, several of the imported presets would fail, so
failing the suite on them would just mean a skipped test. The style guide's
theming section now carries the both-roles rule for anyone adding another
hand-authored theme (and its pointer to `globals.css`, which moved to
`packages/web-ui/styles/themes.css`, is corrected).

## Upgrading

Nothing to do, no migrations, no config. The theme appears in the picker on
next load; it's opt-in like every other, and the default stays `clean-slate`.
